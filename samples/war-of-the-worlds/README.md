# Testscan: The War of the Worlds

This 326-page scan is a full-length PDF fixture for extraction and audiobook tests. The source PDF contains scanned page images plus an OCR text layer; the app does not run OCR on image-only PDFs.

## Provenance and rights

- Work: *The War of the Worlds*, H. G. Wells, first published in 1898.
- Scan: Harper & Brothers edition held by Brigham Young University; digitized by the Internet Archive in 2011.
- Source record: [Internet Archive item](https://archive.org/details/warofworlds1898well).
- Original scan metadata: [Wikimedia Commons file page](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:War_of_the_Worlds.djvu), marked Public Domain Mark 1.0. It records the author’s death in 1946 and the edition’s 1898 publication date.
- In Germany, § 64 UrhG sets the term at 70 years after the author’s death; the term for this work expired at the end of 2016. See [§ 64 UrhG](https://www.gesetze-im-internet.de/urhg/__64.html).
- Downloaded text-PDF derivative: [Internet Archive PDF](https://archive.org/download/warofworlds1898well/warofworlds1898well.pdf).

The file is kept here as a public-domain test fixture. Its scanned library markings and OCR mistakes are part of the input being tested.
